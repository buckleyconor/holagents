# Guide quality (holistic)

One overall judgment of the finished guide as a single deliverable —
the final pass before rename/publish. Score `overall` 1–5.

Anchors: 5 = reads like the style-corpus samples at their best:
the introduction promises exactly what the modules deliver, pacing
holds across modules, the summary is honest, and a new user finishes
the lab with the claimed outcomes. 4 = publishable; one module
under-delivers relative to its promise. 3 = a new user finishes with
a visible gap between promise and delivery. 2 = the guide teaches
pieces of a lab, not the lab. 1 = not a coherent guide.

Format mechanics are the linter's job — do not dock for those. Judge
promise/delivery alignment, cross-module continuity, and total
believability. Findings must name the module(s) and the specific
promise that is broken.
