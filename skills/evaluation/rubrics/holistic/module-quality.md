# Module quality (holistic)

One overall judgment: would a reader complete this module
top-to-bottom, first try, without going back? Score `overall` 1–5.

Anchors: 5 = flows naturally from its starting state; a first-time
reader is never stuck, surprised, or lost; pacing feels right;
checkpoints land at natural rest points. 4 = one minor wobble a
careful reader bridges alone. 3 = a genuine gap: a state is referenced
that the prior steps never established, or a step order fights the
reader. 2 = the module requires re-reading or hunting for context.
1 = incoherent.

Judge experience, not mechanics (step/technical rubrics cover those).
Findings must name the exact step and the moment a reader would get
stuck.
