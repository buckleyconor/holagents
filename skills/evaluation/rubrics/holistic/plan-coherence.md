# Plan coherence (holistic)

One overall judgment: does this plan read as a single coherent
learning story? Score `overall` 1–5.

Anchors: 5 = a learner who sees only the plan title + objectives can
predict the shape of the experience, and every module earns its place;
4 = coherent with at most one weak module boundary; 3 = the story
works but a module is out of place or an objective is unowned;
2 = multiple modules do not serve the stated objectives;
1 = a list of modules, not a plan.

Judge the arc, not the completeness (completeness is the checklist
rubric's job). Findings must name the specific module/objective that
breaks coherence.
