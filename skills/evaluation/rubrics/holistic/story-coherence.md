# Story coherence (concept level)

Read the concept as one document. Does it hold together as a story someone
would want to walk through?

## Criteria

### arc-holds

The beats form an arc — a baseline is established, something is done, an
outcome is revealed — rather than a list of things the product can do. Each
beat earns the next.

### concept-to-sizing-fit

Every beat is something a small demo environment could plausibly show. The
story does not quietly require a footprint no lab pool could run many of.

### scope-is-fenced

The problem, the beats, the success criteria and the non-goals describe one
lab, not three. Nothing in the beats falls outside what the success criteria
claim, and nothing in the success criteria is unsupported by a beat.

### it-would-land

Taken whole, a reader can see why someone would spend an hour on this and
what they would tell a colleague afterwards. The aha moment is genuinely a
moment, not a summary.
