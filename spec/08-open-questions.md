# 08 — Open questions & assumptions

Everything the spec had to decide where the brief was silent is listed below.
**Assumptions** were made and baked into the design; if one is wrong, the impact is
stated. **Decisions to confirm** need a human answer before the relevant milestone
(starting from the M-number given).
